Recovering

After you get hit you need a moment to shake it off before you can attack again (you can still guard). After a heavy attack there’s also a short wait before your next attack.

Knockdowns

Big hits that aren’t guarded knock the opponent down: roundhouses, the Sky Drop Kick, slide kicks, the Vanish Drop Kick, sweeps and more. A low kick can still hit someone lying down, once. Getting up takes a moment and you can’t be hit while rising. Tap ← or → while down to roll that way instead, or press an attack while rising to strike as soon as you’re up.

Light, heavy and guarding

Light attacksFast and safe when guarded. You recover first, so you can guard again in time.U J
Heavy attacksEach fighter has their own heavy punch and heavy kick with longer reach. They hit harder and drain the guard meter, but recover slowly when guarded, so a light attack can punish them up close.I K
Guard meterThe thin bar under your health. Guarding drains it, heavy attacks and specials most of all. At zero your guard breaks and you’re left dizzy. It refills slowly while you aren’t guarding.
MP gaugeOne gauge at the bottom of the screen. Hold P to charge it; it also fills a little when you deal or take damage.

Online battle

Pick Online on the title screen, enter your username (required) and choose your fighter. Random match pairs you with whoever else is waiting; to play a friend, one of you creates a room with a number and the other joins with the same number. The host plays on the left. Esc leaves the match. Online play needs the page hosted on the web (for example GitHub Pages).

CPU vs CPU

Pick CPU vs CPU on the title screen (or press C there) to watch the two CPUs fight a full match. The fighter you pick plays on the left and the CPU opponent you pick on the right. They play the sample combos: hit-confirming jabs into launchers, punishing with counters, chasing launched or stunned opponents, and the name of each combo shows under the hit counter. Press Esc to pause or quit.

Training mode

Pick Training on the title screen. Nobody gets knocked out and health refills by itself. The panel at the top shows the frame advantage of the last attack that connected; a plus number means the attacker recovers first. The dummy starts out guarding automatically. Use the buttons above the game or the keys: R resets both fighters to their start positions with full health, T switches the dummy between standing still, guarding, crouching, jumping and fighting back, and M turns the MP refill on or off.

→ → means tap toward your opponent twice, then press the button; ← ← means tap away from them twice. → means toward your opponent and ← means away, so they flip when you switch sides. Punch is U (light) or I (heavy) and Kick is J (light) or K (heavy); each one gives a different special. Holding ↓ when you press the button gives the rising version of that special (see each fighter).

打ち上げと追撃

打ち上げた相手が空中にいる間、U・I・Kは追撃技になる。近くでジャンプすると空中連続技。1回のコンボで打ち上げられるのは2回まで。3回目はほとんど浮かない（「打ち上げ上限」）。気絶は打ち上げに数えない。

ダウンと起き上がり

大きな攻撃を受けるとダウンする。倒れた相手には下段蹴りが一度だけ当たる。起き上がりは無敵。倒れている間に←か→で転がって移動、起き上がり中に攻撃を押すとすぐ反撃。

ノックバック

攻撃を受けると後ろに押し出される。壁際で相手が下がれないときは、その分だけ攻撃側が押し戻される。空中でも弱攻撃より強攻撃のほうが大きく飛ぶ。

オンライン対戦

タイトル画面でオンラインを選び、ユーザー名（必須）を入力してキャラを選ぶ。ランダムマッチは待っている相手と自動で対戦。友だちと遊ぶときは、片方が番号を決めてルームを作り、もう片方が同じ番号で参加する。ルームを作った側が左。Escで退出。オンライン対戦はWeb上（GitHub Pagesなど）に置いたページで動作します。

CPU対CPUとトレーニング

タイトル画面でCPU対CPUを選ぶ（またはC）と、選んだ2人のCPUが対戦する。コンボ見本のルートを使い、コンボ名がヒット数の下に表示される。トレーニングではKOされず、体力は自動で回復。上の表示で最後の攻撃の有利フレームが見られる。ダミーは最初から自動でガードする。Rで位置リセット、Tでダミーの動き切替、MでMP回復のオン・オフ。

→→は相手の方向に2回、←←は反対方向に2回押してからボタン。→は相手の方向、←はその反対なので、左右が入れ替わると逆になる。ボタンと一緒に↓を押すと、その必殺技の「上昇版」が出る。
